## Design Context

### Users
Fans, followers, and collaborators of GACETA — an independent Argentine urban music label. Users arrive primarily from social media (Instagram, TikTok) on mobile, curious about artists and shows. Secondary audience: music industry contacts viewing on desktop. Context: discovery-mode browsing, not task-driven. They want to feel the culture, not just find information.

### Brand Personality
**Raw. Confident. Cultural.**

GACETA is an independent label that plays at the level of majors — editorial, bold, no apologies. The site should feel like a magazine shoot, not a portfolio. Energy of urban Argentine music: direct, high-contrast, kinetic.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Theme**: Dark. Near-black (`#0a0a0a`) with noise texture. White text. Editorial.
- **Accent**: `#dee5a0` (acid gold/yellow-green) — the GACETA signature. Used sparingly for maximum impact.
- **Secondary accent**: `#911e1e` (deep red) for drama.
- **Typography**: Inter (bold, condensed, tight tracking) as primary. Instrument Serif italic for contrast/emotion moments.
- **Motion**: GSAP + Lenis. Intentional, not decorative. Wipes, clip-path reveals, scroll-driven sequences.
- **Anti-references**: Generic music label sites, dark mode + glowing neon, gradient text, glassmorphism.
- **References**: NY editorial magazines, European art direction, COLORS magazine, high-contrast fashion photography.

### Design Principles
1. **Every interaction earns its place** — no animation unless it communicates something
2. **Black and white is the canvas, acid gold is the punctuation** — color is scarce and therefore powerful
3. **Scale creates hierarchy** — 18vw text next to 10px mono labels is the signature contrast
4. **Mobile-first, but desktop is cinematic** — the experience upgrades on larger screens
5. **Culture over decoration** — nothing generic, nothing that could be mistaken for another site
